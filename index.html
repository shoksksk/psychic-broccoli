GCIT Tech Fest 2026

One day of hacking, talks and demos for GCIT students.

About the event

GCIT Tech Fest brings students from every programme together for a day of building and learning. You can join a 6-hour mini hackathon, attend short talks from industry guests, and show your projects at the demo corner. Beginners are welcome – no prior hackathon experience is needed.
